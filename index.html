Read before use. These numbers measure disproportionality in spontaneous reports: whether a reaction is reported more often with a drug than with other drugs. They do not show that the drug causes the reaction, do not estimate how often it happens, and are not medical advice. Method & limitations

Source: FDA Adverse Event Reporting System (FAERS), now the FDA Adverse Event Monitoring System (AEMS), quarterly files 2024Q3 to 2026Q2. The FDA does not endorse this project.
